1Open or install the app

Shop Calculator works in any modern browser and needs no account. You can use it straight from the link, or install it so it opens like a normal app, even without internet.

DeviceHow to install
Android phoneOpen the link in Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu, then Install app (or Add to Home screen). Or install it from Google Play when it is available.
iPhone / iPadOpen the link in Safari, tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
Windows / MacOpen the link in Chrome or Edge and click the small install icon at the right end of the address bar. Or just keep it as a bookmark.
After the first time it opens, it works offline. Your shop name, items and sales stay on that device only.

2First-time setup

Do this once. It takes about two minutes, and the app remembers everything.

  1. Choose Sales mode. At the top, tap Sales. (Calc is a plain calculator.) On a computer or tablet both views are always shown side by side.
  2. Type your shop name in the bar under the title. It is printed on every bill and PDF.
  3. Pick your currency from the drop-down (for example INR, AED, KWD, USD). Decimals and the symbol adjust automatically, for example 3 decimals for KWD.
  4. Set your usual tax rate. Tap TAX, type the rate (for example 5), then tap PRICE to move on. The app keeps this rate for every new item. Use 0 if you do not charge tax.
  5. Shop details (optional). Tap the small card icon next to the currency to add your logo, address, phone and tax number. They print at the top of your bills and reports. See Shop details below.
  6. Sound. The speaker button turns key clicks on or off.
Do not press = while you are only setting the tax rate. In Sales mode = adds the current item to the sale. Tap another box (like PRICE) instead.

3Make your first sale

Each item is one line. Fill in what you need, then press =.

  1. Item name (optional) and unit such as kg, pcs, box. The app suggests items you sold before and fills in their last price.
  2. Tap QTY and type the quantity. Tap PRICE and type the price of one unit.
  3. Discount (optional). Tap DISC and type a number. Use the % / Amount switch to choose whether it is a percentage or a fixed amount taken off this item.
  4. Tax (optional). Your saved rate is already filled in. Tap TAX to change it for this item.
  5. Press the green = key. The item flies into Current Sale. Repeat for the next item.

Fixing mistakes

  • Change an item: tap its row, change the numbers, then press = to update it. (Tap cancel to leave it unchanged.)
  • Remove an item: tap the × at the end of its row.
  • Undo removes the last item added. CE clears what you are typing. AC clears the whole sale.

Overall discount

To take a fixed amount off the whole bill (for example a coupon), type it in the Overall Discount box below the list. It cannot make the total negative.

4Shop details on your bills (optional)

Make your bills look professional. Tap the card icon next to the currency, fill in what you want, and tap Save. Everything here is optional, and anything you leave empty is simply not printed.

  • Address (up to 3 lines), Phone and Tax / registration no. (for example your GST or VAT number).
  • Logo: tap Choose logo and pick a picture (PNG or JPG work best). It is shrunk automatically. Tap Remove to take it off.
  • They appear on the PDF bill, the shared text bill (WhatsApp, Email, copy), the PDF report and the top of the Excel report.
  • Your shop name is still typed in the bar above the calculator.
The details and logo stay on your device, like everything else. Nothing is uploaded.

5Save the sale

When the customer has paid, tap the green Save sale button. The sale is recorded in your history and the screen clears, ready for the next customer.

A sale is only added to your history and daily totals when you tap Save sale. Share first if the customer wants the bill, then save.

7Sales history and reports

Tap the clock icon next to the currency to see what you have saved.

  • Daily total: tap Today to see how many sales you made and the total.
  • Any dates: set From and To, or use Yesterday, Last 7 days, This month, All.
  • See the items in a sale: tap the sale in the list. You can delete a wrong entry there.
  • Reports: PDF report and Excel report cover the dates you chose, with daily totals and every sale. The Excel file also lists every item sold. On a phone the file opens your share menu so you can send it or save it to Drive.
History is kept for 2 months on this device, then older sales are removed automatically. It is not backed up, and clearing the browser data or removing the app deletes it. Export an Excel report regularly if you want permanent records.

8Handy tips

  • Repeat items are quick. Start typing a name you used before, pick it from the list, and the price, discount and tax are filled in for you.
  • Just need a calculator? Tap Calc for a plain calculator.
  • On a computer you can type numbers with the keyboard; Enter works like =.
  • Big amounts: the display handles up to 8 digits (99,999,999). Anything bigger shows an error message.
  • Switching currency only affects new sales. History and reports show one currency at a time, and tell you if sales in another currency exist.
  • Need this guide again? Tap the round ? button at the top right of the app at any time. It opens this guide without losing your sale.
  • Your data is private. Nothing is sent to us or anyone else. Shop name, items and history live only on your device.

9Questions

The item will not add when I press =

Both Qty and Price must be greater than zero. A message under the keypad tells you what is missing.

My history is empty

Sales appear only after you tap Save sale. Also check the dates at the top, and that the currency matches the one you sold in.

The Excel or PDF report says "No sales in this date range"

Widen the dates (try All) and check the currency selected in the app.

My logo or address does not show on the PDF

Open Shop details (the card icon next to the currency), check the details and tap Save. Then create the PDF again. If you share the bill by WhatsApp or Email, close and reopen the Share menu so it picks up the new details.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Once it has opened one time online, everything works offline, including PDF and Excel reports.

I changed phones. Where did my sales go?

Data stays on the device where it was entered. Export an Excel report before changing phones to keep your records.

The app looks out of date

Close it fully and open it again while online. It updates itself the next time it connects.
