## Full description (max 4000)

Shop Calculator is a fast, simple register for small shops. Ring up a sale, apply discounts and tax,
and send the bill to your customer — all on your phone, even with no internet.

POS. BILLING. SALES.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Calculator and Sales modes — a plain calculator, or a full sale register
• Add items with name, unit, quantity and price
• Discount per item (percentage or flat amount) plus an overall discount on the whole sale
• Tax per item, with subtotal, tax and grand total always visible
• Grand total written out in words, cheque style
• 15 currencies, with the right number of decimals for each
• Remembers your recent and most-used items and fills in the price for you
• Share the bill as a PDF, or send it by WhatsApp or Email
• Put your logo, address, phone and tax number on every bill and report (all optional)
• Save every sale and see daily totals
• Choose any from–to date range and export a PDF or Excel report
• Works fully offline; light and dark themes

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no ads, no tracking. Your sales and prices stay on your phone. Sales history is kept for
2 months and then removed automatically.
