Shop Calculator — Privacy Policy

POS. BILLING. SALES. · by bconnTech · Last updated: 7 November 2026

Shop Calculator is a billing and sales calculator for small businesses. This policy explains what the app does with information. The short version: everything stays on your device, and we do not collect anything.

Information we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. bconnTech does not receive, store or have access to any data you enter.

Information stored on your device

To work properly the app saves the following on your device only: your shop name, preferred currency, tax rate and sound setting; a short list of recently and frequently used item names and prices; and the sales you choose to save to the in-app history (kept for 2 months, then deleted automatically). You can delete saved sales at any time from the History screen, and uninstalling the app removes all of it.

Sharing

The app never sends your data anywhere by itself. If you tap Share, WhatsApp, Email, PDF or Excel, the receipt or report you chose is handed to the app or service you pick (for example WhatsApp, your email app or Google Drive). From that point it is handled under that service's own privacy policy.

Permissions

The app does not ask for access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files. It works without an internet connection.

Children

The app is a general business tool and is not directed at children. It collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
